deliveryCharge()

If the order ammount is ₦50,000 or more, delivery is free regardless of distance. Otherwise, if the distance is 5 km or less, the delivery fee is ₦1,500. Otherwise, if the distance is greater than 5 km but less than or equal to 15 km, the delivery fee is ₦3,000. Otherwise, the delivery fee is ₦5,000. Examples: deliveryCharge(60 000, 20) -- 0 deliveryCharge(30 000, 5) -- 1500 deliveryCharge(20 000, 10) -- 3000 deliveryCharge(15 000, 16) -- 5000 deliveryCharge(50 000, 3) -- 0 deliveryCharge(30 000, 15) -- 3000
